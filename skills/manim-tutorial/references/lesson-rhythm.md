# Lesson rhythm

Plan beats as a sequence of conceptual changes, not sentences. Keep one new idea per beat. Build anticipation before a transformation, make the transformation visible while narrating it, then hold long enough for recognition. Use a compact recap beat to bind the visual effect to the mathematical statement.

Time the visual action independently from the narration. Use these ranges as starting points:

- Fade, indicate, or reveal a label in about 0.3–0.6 seconds.
- Write a short formula in about 0.5–0.9 seconds.
- Show a major geometric transformation in about 0.8–1.4 seconds.
- Keep a multi-stage reveal to about 1.5–2.2 seconds in total.

Let the beat context hold the completed visual while the remaining narration plays. Do not slow a transition merely to consume `beat.duration`; a half-written formula or half-transformed object should not linger. Extend motion across most of a beat only when the changing motion itself carries information. After a major result, add a short silent pause outside the beat when recognition needs more time.
